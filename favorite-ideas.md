# Favorite Dish Ideas

A running list of dish ideas worth trying. Not full recipes — just concepts to develop later.

## Chipotle Hot-Honey Smoked Chicken Thighs

Fits the wheelhouse: smoker + chipotle + hot honey. Smoke bone-in thighs low, then glaze in the last 20 minutes.

- **Approx total time:** 2–2.5 hrs (mostly hands-off)
- **Core:** chicken thighs, chipotle sauce as rub base, honey + cayenne glaze
